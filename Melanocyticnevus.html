Melanocytic Nevus

What is it?

Moles are clusters of melanocytes (pigment cells) that form on the skin.

Causes

Genetics and sun exposure.

Symptoms

Small, pigmented spots (brown, black, or tan). Flat or raised, smooth or rough in texture.

Precautions

Use sunscreen to prevent changes in moles. Monitor for sudden changes using the ABCDE rule (Asymmetry, Border, Color, Diameter, Evolving).

Doctor’s Advice

If a mole changes, a biopsy may be needed. Surgical removal if cancerous transformation is suspected.
