Dermatofibroma

What is it?

A dermatofibroma is a benign skin tumor caused by excess growth of fibroblast cells.

Causes

May develop after minor skin trauma (insect bites, small cuts).

Symptoms

Firm, reddish-brown nodules, often on the legs. A dimple sign when pinched.

Precautions

Avoid trauma or scratching the lesion. Monitor for sudden changes in size or color.

Doctor’s Advice

No treatment required unless painful. Can be surgically removed if necessary.
