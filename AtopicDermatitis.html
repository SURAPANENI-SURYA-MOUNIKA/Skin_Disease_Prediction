Atopic Dermatitis

What is it?

Atopic dermatitis is a chronic inflammatory skin condition that leads to itchy, dry, and inflamed skin. It is often linked to allergies, asthma, and genetic factors.

Causes

Genetic predisposition (family history of eczema, asthma, or hay fever). Immune system dysfunction leading to an overactive inflammatory response. Environmental triggers like pollen, dust, pet dander, stress, and weather changes.

Symptoms

Dry, itchy, inflamed skin, usually on the hands, face, elbows, and knees. Red rashes, which may ooze, crust, or thicken over time. Flare-ups triggered by allergens, soaps, wool, stress, and climate changes.

Precautions

Use gentle, fragrance-free moisturizers and soaps. Avoid hot showers and scratching the affected area. Identify and eliminate known triggers. Wear loose, cotton clothing to prevent irritation.

Doctor’s Advice

Topical corticosteroids and antihistamines to control inflammation and itching. Immunomodulators like dupilumab for severe cases. Light therapy (phototherapy) if topical treatments fail.
